About Us

Who We Are

Ladies School of Driving is a UK-based driving school dedicated to providing a safe, supportive, and empowering environment for female learner drivers — and for those who want to build a career as a professional driving instructor.

We are a female-led driving school staffed exclusively by DVSA-approved female instructors. Every member of our team is DBS checked, professionally trained, and confident learning environment where every pupil feels valued and supported.

Our school was founded on the belief that driving tuition should be accessible, patient, and personalised — not rushed or one-size-fits-all. Whether you are picking up a steering wheel for the very first time, returning to driving after a break, or aiming to qualify as a driving instructor yourself, we have a programme built around your needs.

🎯 What We Offer

Beginner lessons, manual and automatic tuition, intensive courses, night driving, defensive driving, EV training and full ADI instructor qualification training — all delivered by qualified female instructors.

👩‍🏫 Our Instructors

Every instructor is DVSA-approved, DBS checked, and trained in anxiety-aware teaching techniques. We take care selecting instructors who are not only highly qualified but patient, encouraging, and genuinely committed to each pupil's success.

🌟 Our Mission

To provide the highest standard of driving tuition in a safe, female-friendly environment — building lasting road confidence in learner drivers.

Become a Driving Instructor

ADI Qualification
Training Programme

We train women who want to turn driving into a career. Our full ADI training programme guides you through all three mandatory DVSA qualification parts — blending online learning with in-person coaching so you can fit training around your life.

As a PDI (Trainee Licence holder), you can start earning money teaching learners before you've even fully qualified — giving you income while you complete your training.

Start Earning While You Train

Once you pass Part 1 and Part 2, you may be eligible for a Trainee Driving Instructor (PDI) licence — letting you teach learners and earn real income during your training period.

Enquire About ADI Training

Part 1 — Theory & Hazard Perception

DVSA Theory Examination

A 100-question multiple-choice test on road procedure, driving theory, and a hazard perception video test. Our online modules and mock tests get you test-ready.

Mock theory testsHazard perception practiceProgress tracking

Part 2 — Advanced Driving Ability

Eyesight & Driving Test

A 1-hour advanced driving test assessed by a DVSA examiner — similar to the Institute of Advanced Motorists standard. We provide intensive 1-on-1 coaching sessions.

Advanced driving coachingMock examiner sessionsRoute familiarisationBespoke feedback

Part 3 — Instructional Techniques

Teaching Ability Test

In-car assessment of your ability to instruct a pupil at two different levels. We run full Part 3 role-play practice sessions to prepare you thoroughly.
