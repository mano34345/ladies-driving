About Us

Who We Are

Ladies School of Driving is a UK-based driving school dedicated to providing a safe, supportive, and empowering environment for female learner drivers — and for those who want to build a career as a professional driving instructor.

We are a female-led driving school staffed exclusively by DVSA-approved female instructors. Every member of our team is DBS checked, professionally trained, and committed to creating a calm, confident learning environment where every pupil feels valued and supported.

Our school was founded on the belief that driving tuition should be accessible, patient, and personalised — not rushed or one-size-fits-all. Whether you are picking up a steering wheel for the very first time, returning to driving after a break, or aiming to qualify as a driving instructor yourself, we have a programme built around your needs.

We serve learner drivers across the UK, offering both in-car practical instruction and a suite of online learning resources to support theory test preparation, hazard perception training, and ongoing skill development — available 24/7, even when our office is closed.

Our mission is simple: to help every woman who comes to us become a safe, confident, and independent driver — and to nurture the next generation of professional female driving instructors through our comprehensive ADI training programme.

🎯 What We Offer

Beginner lessons, manual and automatic tuition, intensive courses, refresher lessons, Pass Plus, night driving, defensive driving, EV training, test preparation, and full ADI instructor qualification training — all delivered by qualified female instructors in dual-control vehicles.

👩‍🏫 Our Instructors

Every instructor at Ladies School of Driving is DVSA-approved, DBS checked, and trained in anxiety-aware teaching techniques. We take great care in selecting instructors who are not only highly qualified but also patient, encouraging, and genuinely committed to each pupil's success.

🌟 Our Mission

To provide the highest standard of driving tuition in a safe, female-friendly environment — building lasting road confidence in learner drivers and developing the professional skills of those who want to shape the next generation of drivers as qualified ADIs.

Pass Plus

A 6-module post-test course covering town driving, motorways, dual carriageways, rural roads, night driving, and adverse weather conditions. Often reduces your insurance premium.

Build confidence driving after dark — hazard spotting, dealing with headlight glare, reduced visibility. A vital skill many new drivers lack. Standalone sessions available.

Become a Driving Instructor

ADI Qualification
Training Programme

We train women who want to turn driving into a career. Our full ADI training programme guides you through all three mandatory DVSA qualification parts — blending online learning with in-person coaching so you can fit training around your life.

As a PDI (Trainee Licence holder), you can start earning money teaching learners before you've even fully qualified — giving you income while you complete your training.

Start Earning While You Train

Once you pass Part 1 and Part 2, you may be eligible for a Trainee Driving Instructor (PDI) licence. This lets you teach learners under supervision and earn real income during your training period.

Enquire About ADI Training

Part 1 — Theory & Hazard Perception

DVSA Theory Examination

A 100-question multiple-choice test on road procedure, traffic signs, driving theory, and a separate hazard perception video test. Our online modules and mock tests get you test-ready.

Online study modules Mock theory tests Hazard perception practice Progress tracking

Part 2 — Advanced Driving Ability

Eyesight & Driving Test

A 1-hour advanced driving test assessed by a DVSA examiner. You must demonstrate a high standard of driving — similar to the Institute of Advanced Motorists standard. We provide intensive 1-on-1 coaching sessions.

Advanced driving coaching Mock examiner sessions Route familiarisation Bespoke feedback

Part 3 — Instructional Techniques

Teaching Ability Test

The final and most complex part — a structured in-car assessment of your ability to instruct a pupil. The examiner role-plays as a learner at two different levels. We run full Part 3 role-play practice sessions.

Role-play coaching PDI licence support Lesson plan templates Post-session feedback
